Our Heritage

From the Heart of East Java

Java & Jawa was born from a simple belief: that world-class furniture shouldn't require a world-class price. The forests and craftspeople of East Java have produced exceptional wood work for generations — we simply connect that skill to the global marketplace.

Our two factories — PT. Java Furniture in Mojokerto and PT. Jawa Lily Furniture in Probolinggo — operate under a unified quality standard while each retaining the local knowledge and techniques that make Javanese craftsmanship unique.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Sustainable Sourcing

We work exclusively with legally sourced timber and actively replant through partnerships with local forestry cooperatives.

Handcraft First

Every mortise, tenon, and finish is executed by hand. We invest in our craftspeople — not in shortcuts.

Uncompromising Quality

Each piece undergoes a multi-point inspection before leaving the factory. If it doesn't meet our standard, it doesn't ship.

Global Reach

With a dedicated warehouse and sales office in Los Angeles, we make ordering from Indonesia as seamless as ordering locally.

How We Work

From Forest to Your Floor

Our process is designed for quality at every step — from raw material selection to final delivery.

01

Material Selection

Timber is hand-selected for grain, density, and moisture content before entering the factory.

02

Milling & Drying

Kiln-dried to the correct moisture level to prevent warping during international shipping.

03

Joinery & Assembly

Traditional mortise-and-tenon and dovetail joints — no staples, no shortcuts.

04

Hand Finishing

Stains, lacquers, and wax applied by hand in our dedicated finishing room. 50+ options available.

05

Quality Control

Multi-point inspection before packing. Any piece that doesn't pass is reworked or rejected.

06

Delivery

Shipped container or via our LA warehouse — we handle logistics so you don't have to.
