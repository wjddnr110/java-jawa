Our Story

Rooted in Java. Built for the World.

PT. Java Furniture and PT. Jawa Lily Furniture are sister factories located in Mojokerto and Probolinggo, East Java — the heartland of Indonesia's finest wood-working tradition.

With operations and a warehouse in Los Angeles, we bridge the craftsmanship of the archipelago directly to retailers, interior designers, and buyers across North America.
